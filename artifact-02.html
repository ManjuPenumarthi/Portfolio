Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning: A Case Study Comparison

ARTIFACT 02

To build real intuition for when to use machine learning versus deep learning, I moved past textbook definitions and grounded the comparison in two deployed systems: Zillow's Zestimate and Oracle NetSuite's Bill Capture. This artifact captures the reasoning I used to determine not just what each approach does, but why a particular modeling strategy is appropriate for a given kind of data and business process.

Description A comparative case study analyzing one machine learning application (Zillow's Zestimate home-valuation model) and one deep learning application (Oracle NetSuite's Bill Capture for invoice processing). For each, I identified the real-world system, explained why its approach fits the problem, and argued why the alternative approach would fail or underperform.
Objective To move beyond definitions and demonstrate practical judgment: given a real business problem and its data, correctly reason through whether classic ML or deep learning is the appropriate approach.
Process I researched how each system works, then built the argument in both directions — why structured, tabular housing data can be handled effectively with statistical and machine-learning methods, and why highly variable document data benefits from models that automatically extract and interpret complex information from invoices.
Tools & Tech Machine Learning, Deep Learning, neural networks, document understanding, OCR, Applied AI/ML Case Analysis
Value Proposition This shows I can translate a core AI/ML distinction into a decision-making framework — the kind of reasoning that matters when scoping whether a project needs a lightweight model or a more complex AI system, rather than defaulting to whichever approach is trendiest.
Unique Value I intentionally chose Bill Capture because it lives in the ERP/finance-automation space I already work in at Corey Construction — connecting the coursework directly to automated document processing and finance workflows relevant to my professional environment.
Relevance As I bring more structured AI evaluation into my NetSuite work, this is the exact judgment call I'll face: matching the modeling approach to the type, structure, and operational use of the data.
References
  • Zillow. What Is a Zestimate? Zillow describes the Zestimate as using statistical and machine-learning models and a neural-network-based model with public, MLS, and user-submitted data.
  • Oracle. NetSuite Applications Suite — Bill Capture. Bill Capture uses OCI Document Understanding to extract key values from vendor bills and learns from user corrections.
  • Oracle. N/documentCapture Module. Oracle documents AI-driven extraction of text, tables, and key-value information from documents.
